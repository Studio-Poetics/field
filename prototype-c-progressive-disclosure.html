≈ 3 min
01

02

03

Open the Sensors panel. Scan the QR code with your phone.

04

Draw a Cue from a sensor to an output. Tilt your phone — the canvas responds.

Four words

🎭
Actor
Anything in your physical surroundings that acts or performs an action.
⚙️
Role
The job an Actor plays — sensing or reacting to things.
➤
Cue
The rule for when and how two Actors act together.
⚡
Mechanism
The exact manner the action plays out — you pick it.
