Four words

Everything else is built from these
🎭
Actor

Anything in your physical surroundings that acts or performs.

⚙️
Role

The job an Actor plays — sensing or reacting to things.

➤
Cue

The rule for when and how two Actors act together.

⚡
Mechanism

The exact way the action plays out — you pick it.
