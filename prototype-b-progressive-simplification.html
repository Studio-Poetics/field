Four words

Everything else is built from these

Start with what's real. 🎭 An Actor is anything in your physical surroundings that acts or performs an action — a plant, a speaker, a phone. Give it a job.

Each Actor has a ⚙️ Role — it can sense something (touch, tilt, light) or react to something (glow, speak, move). Roles are how the world and your canvas talk to each other.

Bring two Actors together with a ➤ Cue — the line you draw between them. A Cue decides when they react to each other. That's the rule you make.

Then pick the ⚡ Mechanism — the exact manner the action plays out. Should touch instantly trigger sound, or build up over time? You choose inside the Cue.

Everything runs locally — no cloud, no account, no tracking.
